Alpi Maps: wrong-way roadsMOCKUP, fake data

Companion of 04-wrong-way-roads.md. Visual language and tokens follow docs/design/mockups.html (round semi-transparent map controls, pills, hairlines). Every frame is shown light, dark and e-ink: colour is never the only signal, entry state is also shape (arrowhead vs bar) and fill (solid vs hatch). Scene: a cyclist heads north (map rotated heading-up) on a main road with three junctions ahead.

1. Follow mode, a junction: today vs a style-only restyle

Left: today, one small arrow per street at z16+ (10 to 16 px, 200 px spacing, 80% opacity). Right: slice 1, denser larger chevrons in an edged band from z15, still direction-agnostic: the style cannot know your heading or the way's far end, so it says "one-way, this way" but not "you may not enter".

3. Follow mode (no route): "next left / next right" chips

Two round chips, one on each side of the screen so the side they talk about is the side they sit on, same recipe as the map buttons (colorMapControl, hairline, 40 dp). Left chip: distance to the first junction ahead with an enterable left exit; right chip likewise. The middle chip is the travel mode (tap cycles walk, bike, car; long press opens the map option).
