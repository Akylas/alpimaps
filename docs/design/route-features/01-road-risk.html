Risky road sections for cycling: mockups (round 2)

MOCKUP, fake data. Companion to 01-road-risk.md. Visual language and tokens copied from ../mockups.html; the two risk colours are new (red = danger token, amber = grade palette). Every level has a pattern so colour is never the only signal. Each screen is shown light, dark and e-ink. Round 2: risk = tagged limit at or above the threshold (default 70 km/h) with no hard separation; ascent and descent share one tile; speed signs on the route style; warning is a banner only.

Route style anatomy

What the style rules draw, layer by layer. All in the poi layer of the items source, above the item, directions and navigation routes.

Route style anatomy · light

Same width as the route under it (@directions_line_width or the item width), 3 px of white casing. Sign = round plate (24 px, red ring 3 px, black number), a billboard label so collision culling applies. Layer poi of the items source, drawn above the item, the directions line and the navigation route.
